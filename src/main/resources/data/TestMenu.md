# Dinner Menu

## Appetizers

* **Edamame Gyoza** ($8) - Pan-seared Japanese-style dumplings filled with soybeans and served with a side of
  wasabi sauce
* **Baba Ghanoush** ($7) - Roasted eggplant dip from the Middle East, served with crispy pita bread and fresh
  parsley
* **Spicy Shrimp Ceviche** ($12) - Fresh shrimp marinated in a spicy lime juice, mixed with onions, peppers, and
  cilantro, served with crispy tortilla chips

* **Veggie Korma** ($15) - A mild and creamy Indian-inspired dish featuring sautéed spinach, bell peppers, and

* **Grilled Octopus** ($20) - Char-grilled octopus tentacles served with a lemon-herb sauce and roasted sweet

* **Stuffed Portobello Mushrooms** ($15) - Earthy portobello mushrooms filled with a mixture of sautéed onions,
